📊 What is aztree?

aztree is a friendly application designed to help you understand Azure billing without needing a degree in accounting or cloud engineering. Whether you're a business owner, a project manager, or just a curious regular user, aztree turns your confusing cloud cost reports into clear, simple summaries.

This tool analyzes your Azure expenses and presents them organized like a tree structure — hence the name "aztree" — making it easy to see where money goes, which services cost the most, and how spending changes over time.

🎯 Why Use aztree?

Azure bills can be intimidating. They feature complex line items, various service names, and regional charges. If you want to know answers to these questions, aztree is for you:

  • 🤔 "Why was I billed more this month?"
  • 💰 "Which service eats my budget?"
  • 📅 "Are our costs trending up or down?"
  • 🔍 "How can I reduce expenses?"

aztree gives clear answers in a simple visual format so you can make informed decisions quickly.

🚀 Getting Started with aztree

Once you launch aztree, here's what to expect:

  1. Login or Import: The app will ask you how you want to access your Azure billing data. You can choose to log in with your Microsoft Azure account (recommended for automatic syncing) or manually import a billing report from your Azure portal.
  2. Choose Date Range: Select the billing period you are interested in. You can view this month, last month, or a custom range.
  3. Review Your Overview: aztree will display a clean summary at the top: total spend, average daily spend, and largest expense category.
  4. Explore the Tree: Click through the tree branches to drill down. For example, click "Compute" → "Virtual Machines" → "Linux VMs" to see exactly what that specific service cost.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is aztree suitable for small businesses?

Absolutely! Aztree scales from individual home users with a single Azure account to small businesses managing multiple subscriptions. The interface is designed to keep things understandable regardless of size.

Do I need technical knowledge to use aztree?

No. aztree is specifically built for people without technical backgrounds. You never see confusing code or raw server names. Everything is presented in plain English and visuals.

Is my billing data safe?

Yes. Aztree processes your billing information locally on your computer. Your Azure billing data never leaves your machine. The app only reads the data you provide — whether that's through login or a file import.

Does aztree support all Azure regions?

Yes, it works with Azure billing data globally. Whether you are in the United States, Europe, Asia, or anywhere else, you'll get clear results.
